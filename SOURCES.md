# Sources and provenance

This repository is public. Record enough information to verify slide claims
without publishing private correspondence, personal contact details or
unapproved course files.

## Source policy

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources for non-trivial factual claims.
- Record private materials through neutral descriptions only.
- Record every final image's source, licence and slide purpose.
- Do not use generated or illustrative images as factual evidence.
- Keep `[Sources]` notes in `src/main.tex` for implemented slides.
